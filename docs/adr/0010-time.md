# ADR-0010: Times through one module

- Status: accepted
- Recorded: 2026-10-30
- In place since: 0.5.1, GetEvent zone check since 0.8.0

## Context

Personal time is UTC, no offset, seconds 00, in whole 5-minute steps. Sessions are Las Vegas local time, PST during the event ([API facts](../api-facts.md)).

## Decision

All conversion and formatting goes through `campus.py`. GetEvent's zone is checked, never its label. Times are computed in America/Los_Angeles, and an event in another zone is refused. Leave times use the walking table and an 11-minute door rule. Walking figures are labelled estimates.

## Alternatives considered

Format times where they are used. Rejected: one wrong offset puts a leave-now block an hour off.

## Consequences

One place to fix a time bug. Walking times are estimates, not routes.
