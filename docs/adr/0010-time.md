# ADR-0010: Times through one module

- Status: accepted
- Recorded: 2026-10-30
- Amended: 2026-11-01, personal time moved from UTC to the event's wall-clock time
- In place since: 0.5.1, GetEvent zone check since 0.8.0

## Context

Personal time is wall-clock time in the event's timezone, no offset, seconds 00, in whole 5-minute steps, on one date. Sessions are Las Vegas local time, PST during the event ([API facts](../api-facts.md)).

Until the spec changed after 2 October 2026, personal time was UTC. re:Seat followed the change on 1 November 2026. The conversion lives in two functions, `to_personal_time` and `from_personal_time`, and every reader of a block now calls them.

## Decision

All conversion and formatting goes through `campus.py`. GetEvent's zone is checked, never its label. Times are computed in America/Los_Angeles, and an event in another zone is refused. Leave times use the walking table and an 11-minute door rule. Walking figures are labelled estimates.

## Alternatives considered

Format times where they are used. Rejected: one wrong offset puts a leave-now block an hour off. When personal time moved from UTC to local time, the conversion changed in one place and the readers switched to it.

## Consequences

One place to fix a time bug. Walking times are estimates, not routes. Blocks written before 1 November 2026 stay in UTC in the official schedule until `reseat guard sync` moves them, or the attendee taps Update in `reseat serve`.
